\textbf{The Developer's Conference}

\href{https://thedevconf.com/}{\textbf{The Developer's Conference}} is the largest tech event happening in Brazil for developers and tech companies. Over 4 years I have delivered \textbf{19 talks and panels} across multiple editions advocating the enterprise design thinking from IBM and the service design sprints practice for startups.

In 2015 after the success of my talks on design, I created the \textbf{Design Thinking Track}, which has been featured in \textbf{14 editions} across \textbf{four cities}, totaling over \textbf{96 hours of content} and reaching out to an audience of more than 1200 developers, managers, and C-level professionals directly and more than 3000 through the online platform. 

My leadership in curating impactful content earned me the \textbf{\href{https://thedevconf.com/rockstar/AdilsonChicoria}{TDC Rockstar Award}}, recognizing my commitment to empowering the tech community and elevating design thinking’s role as collaboration in the industry.\\
